    How it plans

    • Week by week from this Monday. Money lands on the 1st of each month and carries over; chore hours don't: an hour not used in its week is gone.
    • Each project goes: watch listings (calendar time, no budgeted hours; it starts only once the project is next in line for money) → buy (money, then buying hours) → work (chore hours). Hobby hours, like building the radio, are listed but never taken from the budget.
    • A slot is attention: watching listings, buying and working take one; saving up doesn't. Projects take free slots in rank order once their earliest date has passed, and hours go to the highest-ranked project in a slot.
    • A project with a deadline keeps its rank until the deadline gets close: once the time it needs (saving at your monthly rate, watching, chore hours) plus 4 weeks of margin would run out, it jumps to the front.
    • Small buys skip ahead: a purchase of at most one month's budget can go through while a higher-ranked project is still saving; bigger ones wait their turn. Strict order: nothing is bought until every higher-ranked project waiting for money has been bought.
    • Costs are estimates. The list lives in projects.csv next to this page, the defaults in settings.json; the boxes above are a what-if and aren't saved.
